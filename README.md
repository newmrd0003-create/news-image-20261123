# The World in One Day — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026.

## The day

- **COP31 collapses** — climate talks in Türkiye fail with no fossil-fuel deal, as the EU and others reject a text without phase-out language.
- **Tankers ablaze** — oil tankers struck in and near the Strait of Hormuz; Iran's navy says two more caught fire after hitting naval mines.
- **Warship sunk** — suspected Ukrainian sea drones hit the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval base, Libya; the ship capsized, 10 injured.
- **Mercury orbit** — after an eight-year voyage, the European-Japanese BepiColombo spacecraft enters orbit around Mercury, only the second mission ever to do so.
- Also: a Russian drone kills 2 near Kyiv; Lahore chokes at AQI 538; Shuri Castle reopens.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) copies the image this repository holds, `entry.png`, to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a 1024×1024 PNG: a masthead naming the day, a three-panel illustration of the day's biggest stories (the burning tanker, the climate summit hall, the Mercury probe), and four crisp headlines with a footer of further stories. No network is used; every run writes the same bytes.
